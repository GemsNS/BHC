**Prepared for:** BH Contracting Ltd.  
**Project:** 9 Alicia Scott Ave., Mount Uniacke, Nova Scotia  
**Status:** Preliminary quantity estimate; field verification required

The exposed-wall takeoff is based on the permit-set overall dimensions, scheduled opening sizes, and an approximate linear grade transition from upper-floor grade at the principal entrance to lower-slab grade at the garage façade. It is intended to test scope and pricing logic, not to order materials.

The resulting **approximately 30.8-square allowance** is close to the provisional **30-square** note supplied for the Walid job. That agreement is useful as a reasonableness check, but it is not a substitute for field measurement, product-specific coverage calculations, or a signed scope definition.

> Before procurement or fixed-quantity contracting, field-measure all wall segments and rough openings; confirm where cladding stops at grade; confirm membrane, insulation, furring, trims, soffits, flashings, parapets/eaves, starter strips, corners, laps, break-metal, and waste; and confirm whether door installation is included or priced separately.
