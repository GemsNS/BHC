# Walid Warehouse Extension — Façade Design Brief

**Prepared for:** BH Contracting Ltd.  
**Project:** Warehouse Extension, 9 Alicia Scott Ave., Mount Uniacke, Nova Scotia  
**Design basis:** Issued-for-permit geometry, supplied construction photographs, and user-provided façade reference boards  
**Status:** Façade design brief for project review

## Material orientation (fixed)

| Finish | Orientation | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark charcoal cladding | **Vertical board-and-batten** | Primary wall field |
| Warm cedar / wood-grain | **Horizontal** | Accent only (bands, frames, or lower storey depending on concept) |
| Black trim / frames | — | Windows, doors, fascia |
| Garage doors | Horizontal panel lines | Medium grey sectional |

Concepts differ by **where** wood is placed and how battens are used — not by recolouring the same layout.

## Four design concepts

| # | Name | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **Cedar Datum** | Vertical charcoal B&B field + horizontal cedar entrance portal, floor belt, and garage header/drops |
| 02 | **Full Battens** | Charcoal vertical B&B on the entire shell; wood minimized to tiny canopy/soffit accents |
| 03 | **Split Storey** | Horizontal wood wraps the full lower level; vertical charcoal B&B on the upper level and gables |
| 04 | **Framed Bays** | Vertical charcoal B&B field; horizontal wood used as picture-frame surrounds at garage bays and the entrance only |

## Elevation notes (Cedar Datum baseline model)

| Elevation | Charcoal (vertical B&B) | Wood (horizontal) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front / upper entrance | Gable and remaining field | Portal around paired entrance + low base band |
| Garage / shop | Upper wall and gable | Header band + vertical drops between the three sectional doors |
| Left side | Upper field | Floor-transition belt; lower accent follows driveway (man-door near garage corner stays clear of grade) |
| Right side | Upper field | Matching belt without inventing openings |

## Site and grade

Piecewise sloping driveway / grade (not stairs). Grade drops quickly along the side so basement windows and the sidewall man-door remain fully visible above gravel.

## Modeling workflow

Parametric JSCAD source (`01_Model/walid_warehouse.jscad.js`) plus GLB viewer and Manus presentation. Concept PNGs illustrate pattern alternatives; interactive colour remap in the web app is secondary to these layout concepts.
